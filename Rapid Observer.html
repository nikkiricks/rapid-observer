Rapid Observer
UNDP · Crisis Mapping Challenge

Community-led damage signal for the first 48 hours.

An open-source PWA that lets neighbors photograph, classify, and pin damaged infrastructure — online or offline — to feed UNDP's RAPIDA early-recovery workflow.

StackReact PWA · Leaflet · IndexedDB queue · GeoJSON / CSV export
LanguagesEN · ES · FR · AR (RTL) · ZH · RU
TRL4 — clickable prototype with realistic state
ScopeReporter app: onboarding, capture flow, offline queue, dashboard, export
Open the Tweaks panel (toolbar toggle) to swap color theme. The ● online / offline chip in the top-right of the phone toggles connectivity to demo offline queueing & sync.
DEMO PATH
1Pick a language — six UN languages, RTL for Arabic.
2Tap "+ New report" on the home screen.
3Capture a photo — auto-geotagged with GPS & what3words.
4Confirm location by tapping a building footprint or dragging the pin.
5Classify damage — minimal / partial / complete + infra & crisis type.
6Toggle offline (top-right chip) and submit — watch the queue fill.
7View Map tab — pins, heatmap, or clusters of nearby reports.
8Export — CSV or GeoJSON preview from the Home card.
All map tiles via Carto Positron. Footprints synthesized for demo; in production the app would consume Microsoft / Google Open Buildings or local OSM data.
